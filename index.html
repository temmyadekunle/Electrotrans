About us

Read the full story

Electrotrans Engineering & Contractor is a leading heavy equipment engineering and contracting company with over two decades of industry experience, delivering reliable engineering solutions to clients across Nigeria.

We specialize in the sales, purchase, hiring, installation, repair, maintenance and haulage of heavy equipment for construction, industrial, oil & gas, mining and commercial operations. Backed by a team of experienced engineers and technicians, we are committed to quality workmanship, innovative solutions and exceptional customer service.
