About us

The company behind the machines. Two decades of heavy equipment engineering, contracting and haulage experience across Nigeria.

Who we are

Electrotrans Engineering & Contractor is a fully registered Nigerian company, duly incorporated and compliant with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and Nigerian regulations. For over twenty years we have kept heavy equipment fleets working across construction, industrial, oil & gas, mining and commercial operations.

We started with a single workshop and a simple conviction: that Nigerian industry deserves equipment support that is honest, thorough and built on skill. Today we serve clients nationwide — from plant installations along the coast to haulage jobs deep inland — with the same hands-on approach we were founded on.

We are engineers, technicians and logistics specialists first. Our team diagnoses, repairs, overhauls, installs and moves heavy equipment, and we stand fully behind the machines we sell, service and hire out.

Our story

What began as a specialist equipment repair house in Ifo, Ogun State has grown into a full-service partner for heavy machinery. We have overhauled forklifts to factory condition, serviced drilling rigs on site, assembled plant stations for the industrial sector, and safely moved equipment through some of the toughest terrain in the country.

Along the way we learned that reliability is built from three things: the right equipment, honest technical work, and clear communication. Those three principles still guide every job we take on today.

What we do

Electrotrans combines three strengths under one roof:

Equipment

Hire, purchase and sale of excavators, cranes, forklifts, loaders, generators and compressors — inspected, serviced and delivered by our own engineers.

Every machine that leaves our yard has a verifiable service history and has been tested before handover.

Technical services

Mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic engineering — from routine maintenance to full overhauls and on-site diagnostics.

When a machine breaks down, we find the root cause, tell you exactly what is needed, and keep you updated until it is back at work.

Haulage

Heavy equipment transportation and logistics for plant moves, site relocations and load handling anywhere in Nigeria.

We plan the route, arrange the transport, and position your equipment on site — handled end to end.
