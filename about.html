About us

Electrotrans Engineering & Contractor is a leading heavy equipment engineering and contracting company with over two decades of industry experience, delivering reliable engineering solutions to clients across Nigeria.

Who we are

We specialize in the sales, purchase, hiring, installation, repair, maintenance and haulage of heavy equipment for construction, industrial, oil & gas, mining and commercial operations. Our expertise covers the diagnosis, repair and servicing of mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems, ensuring maximum equipment performance, reliability and uptime.

Backed by a team of experienced engineers and technicians, we are committed to providing quality workmanship, innovative solutions and exceptional customer service. Whether you require equipment procurement, technical support, installations or complete engineering and contracting services, Electrotrans Engineering & Contractor is your trusted partner for dependable heavy equipment solutions anywhere in Nigeria.
